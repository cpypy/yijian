1 Introduction

Recurrent neural networks, long short-term memory and gated recurrent neural networks in particular, have been firmly established as state of the art approaches in sequence modeling and transduction problems.

1 引言

循环神经网络，特别是长短期记忆网络和门控循环神经网络，已被牢固确立为 序列建模与转导问题的最先进方法。
Recurrent models typically factor computation along the symbol positions of the input and output sequences. Aligning the positions to steps in computation time, they generate a sequence of hidden states ht, as a function of the previous hidden state and the input for position t hochreiter1997lstm.
循环模型通常沿输入和输出序列的符号位置分解计算。它们将位置与计算时间步对齐， 生成一系列隐藏状态 ht，作为前一个隐藏状态 与位置 t 输入的函数 hochreiter1997lstm。
The Transformer follows this overall architecture using stacked self-attention and point-wise, fully connected layers for both the encoder and decoder.
Transformer 沿用这一整体架构，在编码器和解码器中都使用堆叠的自注意力层 和逐位置全连接层。
